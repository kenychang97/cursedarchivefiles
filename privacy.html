Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

"Cursed Archive Files Publisher" (the "App") is a private publishing tool used only by its owner for the owner's own TikTok account.

Data we access

  • Basic TikTok account info needed to post (open id, display name).
  • Permission to upload and publish videos (video.publish) and to read the owner's own video statistics (video.list).

How it is used and stored

Access tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer and used only to publish the owner's videos and read their performance. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Other people

The App does not collect data about viewers or any other TikTok users.

Deletion

Revoking the App in TikTok settings ends all access; the owner deletes the stored tokens from their computer.

Contact

The owner via the Cursed Archive Files channel page.
